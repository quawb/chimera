Chimera – Rules & Stats

3–5 Models per player, 15 points each, 2 free Abilities

Points & Model Count

Each model costs 15 points and comes with 2 free abilities.

Players select 3–5 models (45–75 points total).

Wounds & Defense

Each model has 4 + Defense Tier wounds.

Defense Tier ranges from 1 to 3 (see Stats table).

Dice System

All rolls use d6.

Round Sequence

  1. Initiative: Each player rolls 1d6. Highest chooses order.
  2. Activation: Players alternate activating unexhausted models. Place an exhaust token after activation.
  3. Maintenance: When all models are exhausted, remove exhaust tokens and start next round.

Activation Sequence

  1. Move Phase: Move up to model’s maximum Move.
  2. Attack Phase: Use ranged or melee weapon. Special actions replace attack if required.
  3. Exhaust Phase: Place an exhaust token; model cannot act again this round.

Attacking & Saving

  1. Roll 1d6 per attack for Shoot or Fight.
  2. Criticals: 6 unmodified = critical hit; 1 unmodified = automatic miss.
  3. Add model’s Shoot or Fight modifier to roll.
  4. Defender rolls 1d6 + modifier per attack:
    • Modified roll beats attack → attack fails.
    • Attack succeeds → subtract weapon damage from Wounds.
  5. Critical Saving: Unmodified 6 = automatic success; 1 = fail. Defender cannot save against unmodified 6 attack.
  6. Ties: Modified defend = attack → attacker wins; modified attack 6 defended with natural 6 → defender saves.
  7. Cannot shoot into or out of combat.

Abilities

Each model may purchase 2 abilities for free, but can only use one per round.

Declare an ability active at the start of model’s activation or when triggered.

Special Actions

  • Disengage: Spend 10" of Move to move 5" away from close-combat attackers. Requires Tier 2 Move.
  • Stand Up: Spend 5" of Move to stand when Down.
  • Recover: Skip Attack Phase to reduce Horror tokens by 1.

Conditions

  • Horror: -1 to rolls per token. Max 3 tokens. Only 1 per phase.
  • Down: Model is down, cannot act until Standing Up. -1 to Defense Rolls.

Cover

Partially concealed models or models >15" from attacker gain +1 to Defense rolls.
